✦ Horological Discipline in Syntax

Conventional syntax themes use jarring high-contrast primary colors (saturated neon pink, cyan, yellow) that cause retinal fatigue. In TAO, syntax tokens are tuned like watchmaker alloys: thermally blued steel, brass wheels, and titanium bridges that respect sustained focus over hours of deep work.

✦ Taoist Equilibrium (留白 & Restraint)

Not every symbol demands attention. Operators and punctuation remain quiet hairline ink, while comments adopt a muted titanium slate that recedes into the drafting plane until intentionally read. Whitespace is an active structural element.
